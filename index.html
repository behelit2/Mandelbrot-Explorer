Rendering & stepping

Camera steps undo or replay recorded views. At the end of the timeline, forward creates a new zoom step.

Resolution drops during movement and refines when you stop. Detail normally increases with zoom depth. Color animation reuses the last calculation instead of recalculating the fractal.

Take the controls

  • Drag: disconnect the flight camera and pan.
  • Pinch / mouse wheel: zoom around your fingers or pointer.
  • Double-tap: zoom into the tapped location.
  • Free camera: parks the flight. Explore independently, then use Reconnect to return.
  • Play in free camera: dive straight into your chosen point.
  • Back / forward: pause and step through camera history, or change iteration detail.

Under the surface

Deep views use expanding-precision BigInt reference orbits in a background worker and scaled perturbation rendering. This avoids the ordinary floating-point zoom wall. Boundary flight has no preset zoom-depth limit; actual speed, accuracy, and resolvable detail remain limited by your device and iteration budget. The endless tour revisits a collection of launch sites.

WebGL 2 accelerates compatible phones. A lower-resolution CPU canvas renderer takes over if WebGL is unavailable. Nothing is downloaded or sent to a server.
